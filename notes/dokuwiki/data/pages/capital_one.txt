After sending the money via Zelle in the CapitalOne mobile app, I wanted to take a screenshot of the confirmation page and send it to the recipient via WhatsApp. But I got an error message saying that the CapitalOne app does not allow taking screenshots within the app.

This is very inconvenient.

Workaround: Login into the account via browser (capitalone.com) on the desktop and take a screenshot of the transaction and send it. Not ideal but it worked.
